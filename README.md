# 🧭 TripCraft — Hyper-Local & Multi-Day Itinerary & Multi-Modal Transit Engine

> **The intelligent trip planning app for travelers who know where they want to go, but have no idea what places to visit inside, where to stay cheap, what legendary food to eat, how long to spend at each spot, and how to travel step-by-step between every stop.**

---

## 🌟 New Feature: Smart Locality & Place Explorer Search

You can now **search ANY place or locality** (both inside Bangalore like *Indiranagar*, *Malleshwaram*, *Koramangala*, *Church Street*, *Jayanagar*, *Vasanth Nagar*, *HSR Layout*, *Whitefield* or outside Karnataka like *Coorg*, *Ooty*, *Nandi Hills*, *Gokarna*, *Hampi*, *Munnar*, *Alleppey*, *Varkala*, *Pondicherry*, or **any custom entered town**).

When you search:
1. **Instant Place Analysis**:
   - Vibe & atmosphere breakdown
   - Recommended stay time (e.g. *Half Day (3–6 hrs)* vs *2–3 Days*)
   - Best companion suitability (*Girlfriend Date*, *Guy Squad*, *Solo*, etc.)
2. **Suggested Places to Visit Inside & Nearby**:
   - 3 to 5 curated attractions/spots with recommended duration (e.g. *2 hrs*) and highlight summaries
3. **How to Travel & Transit**:
   - Metro connectivity (Purple Line / Green Line), Rapido fares, or highway / train / bus routes
4. **Where to Stay Cheap**:
   - Curated backpacker hostels (e.g. *Locul Central ₹650*, *Zostel ₹650*) and verified budget homestays
5. **Must-Try Local Food**:
   - Signature local dishes (e.g. *CTR Benne Dosa*, *Glen's Bakehouse Red Velvet*, *Toit Pizza & Cider*)
6. **One-Click Itinerary Generation**:
   - Center a tailored 1-day or multi-day trip directly around the searched locality!
